The Intermediate agility class is designed for handler and dog teams who are actively competing, or preparing to compete, in agility trials. The format offers a structured, goal-oriented curriculum that supports continued skill development and prepares teams for success in the competition ring.

Class focus

  • Honing obstacle performance for speed, accuracy and consistency
  • Advancing handling techniques — proper execution of cues, obstacle versus handler focus, and obstacle independence
  • Developing strategies for course analysis and efficient path execution
  • Enhancing teamwork, timing and off-leash communication under distraction and trial-like conditions
  • Building ring readiness through simulated competition exercises and coursework
  • Preparing for competition through effective handling and strategy

Who should enroll

  • Teams who have completed Advanced Beginners, and/or instructor approval
  • Dogs should be competent on all agility obstacles, including contacts and 12 weave poles
  • Dogs must be able to work independently off leash in a high-energy environment
  • Handlers should have experience with basic handling maneuvers and course flow

Class structure

  • Weekly sessions focused on specific skill themes and progressive sequences
  • Opportunities for individualized coaching within a group setting
  • Weekly homework required to reinforce learning
  • Teams are re-evaluated at the end of each 16-week session to ensure the best class fit

Homework

Weekly homework is required in this class. Reinforcing each week's theme between sessions is what turns a skill practised once into one your dog can perform under trial pressure.

Details

  • Length: 16 weeks
  • Cost: $250, including annual club membership
  • Class size: limited to 12 dog and handler teams
  • Where: Faith Family Church, 11530 E Queen Creek Road, Chandler, AZ 85286
