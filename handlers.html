Emphasis and goals

Handlers is our most advanced class, focused on the handling skills that separate a clean run from a fast one. Expect to work sequences at the Masters level and to be pushed on the movements that show up in championship courses.

  • Advanced handling skills and movement
  • Backsides
  • Threadles
  • Serpentines
  • Advanced distance work — linear, sequencing (gamble), layering
  • 180's and 270's, wraps and turns
  • Crosses and lead outs
  • Obstacle discrimination
  • Strengthening weave work — drive, difficult approaches and exits
  • Practice sequences at the Masters level
  • Strategies for running courses as a team

Prerequisite

Handlers is intended for teams already competing at the Advanced level or higher. Completion of Intermediate or equivalent experience, plus a successful evaluation.

Details

  • Length: 16 weeks
  • Cost: $250, including annual club membership
  • Class size: limited to 6 dog and handler teams
  • Where: Faith Family Church, 11530 E Queen Creek Road, Chandler, AZ 85286
