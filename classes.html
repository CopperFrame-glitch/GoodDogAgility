Classes

Dog agility classes in Chandler.

Two sessions a year, four levels, sixteen weeks each. $250 including your annual club membership.

Choose your level.

Never done agility before? Start with Beginners. Familiar with the obstacles but still building skills? Advanced Beginners. Coming from another club? Email us and we'll usually schedule an evaluation to place you correctly.

1

Beginners

Mondays · 6:30–7:45 PM Closed

Introduces dogs to the obstacles while you build the foundation: start-line stays, coming to hand, and taking direction from you. No experience needed.

See the class
2

Advanced Beginners

Mondays · 7:45–9:00 PM Full

Continues obstacle work for teams already comfortable with jumps, teeter, dog walk, A-frame and weave poles.

See the class
3

Intermediate

Tuesdays · 6:30–7:45 PM Full

For teams competing or preparing to compete. Obstacle performance for speed and accuracy, course analysis, off-leash communication and ring readiness.

See the class
4

Handlers

Tuesdays · 7:45–9:00 PM Full

Advanced handling for teams competing at Advanced level or higher — backsides, threadles, distance work and Masters-level sequences.

See the class

Join the waiting list

Email secretary@gooddog.org with your name, your dog's name and age, and the level you want. We'll also contact you if a place comes free this session.

Requirements

  • Dogs must be at least 9 months old before the first class meeting
  • A crate or exercise pen is required — see What to Expect
  • Dogs should be able to cope in a group setting without significant stress
  • Dogs must be healthy, in good physical condition and current on vaccinations

Where we train

Faith Family Church, 11530 E Queen Creek Road, Chandler, AZ 85286. The training field is fully fenced and gated. Enter through the gate — there is no access inside the church and no restrooms on site.

Payment

We accept all major credit cards. Up to 50% of your class fee can be paid with Doggie Bytes earned by volunteering at our shows — check your balance. Junior Handlers aged 17 and under get 30% off, and a further 20% can be paid in Doggie Bytes.
