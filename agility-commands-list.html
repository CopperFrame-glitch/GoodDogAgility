Free reference

Dog agility commands and verbal cues.

A reference list of the verbals we suggest to students. Adapt them however you like — just choose your words before you start training, and stay consistent.

Consistent verbal cues are one of the most useful things you can establish early in agility training. Your dog doesn't care which words you pick, but they care a great deal that you use the same ones every time. Changing a cue mid-training costs you far more than choosing an imperfect one at the start.

A note on choosing your own

Some handlers prefer different words — “tunnel” becomes “through,” “frame” becomes “climb.” That's fine. What matters is that everyone who handles the dog uses the same vocabulary, and that no two cues sound alike. Avoid words that rhyme with each other or with your dog's name.
