First trial?

The Agility Buddy program.

Entering your first trial is genuinely confusing — check-in, height measuring, walk-throughs, running order, gate stewards. Our Agility Buddy program pairs you with an experienced club member who walks you through the whole day so you're not working it out alone at 7am.

Ask about a buddy

Ring crew

Trials run on volunteers.

Every trial we host is staffed by club members timing, scribing, setting bars and running leashes. It's the fastest way to learn how trials actually work, and you earn Doggie Bytes toward class fees and entries.
